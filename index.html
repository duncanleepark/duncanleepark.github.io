Research

Predicting adverse drug reactions from FAERS

Ma'ayan Lab

I'm building a multimodal deep learning pipeline that predicts adverse drug reactions from the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System. It joins chemical fingerprints for about 14.8 million reports with drug-induced gene expression signatures from LINCS L1000, then fuses the two into a shared representation that predicts clinical outcomes.

Most of the work is in the details: sparse matrices too large for memory, extreme class imbalance, and debugging models that quietly stop learning.
